### What I do

I build software end to end: the web apps and APIs people use, the data pipelines behind them, and the AI and ML systems on top. For three years I built, shipped, and maintained web apps for paying clients, from the first requirements call to post-launch support. After that I built AWS data infrastructure as a data engineer. Today most of my work is LLM applications, agents, and applied ML.

### How I do it

- **Cheap checks first.** Rules and validation run before a model, or a person, is ever needed.
- **Measure instead of assuming.** Ablations, tests, and timing logs make the call. One ablation showed the textbook preprocessing recipe more than doubled the error.
- **Structured and testable.** LLM output comes back as validated JSON, results follow typed contracts, and the code ships with real test suites.
- **People decide what matters.** Irreversible steps wait for human approval, and every system reports its status honestly.

### Why I do it

I like taking slow, repetitive work and turning it into a system that runs on its own, so people spend their time on the decisions only they can make. My favorite proof: a client liked the CRM I built for them enough to hire me onto their data team.
